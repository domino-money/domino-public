## Evidence

The producer was exercised at 172,800 blocks and 86,400 credits over a full
24-hour window, compacted exactly into 1,441 records with only the two edge
minutes read from raw history. Price changes and revoked price qualification
invalidate affected output; incomplete coverage never becomes a zero metric
or a complete APR. Raw pages remain available for audit/replay.

Netting gas was measured as independent canonical transaction receipts from
the same local chain snapshot, with equal position, ledger, service cut and
cash effects: ERC-20 general 1,151,557 versus netting 996,399 gas (13.5% less);
native general 1,100,903 versus netting 949,520 (13.8% less). These are local
fixture measurements, not a guarantee for every position or current gas price.
The final V4 vault runtime is 24,263 bytes and engine runtime 21,902 bytes;
the repository enforces runtime and constructor-size limits.

Independent review corrected native refunds to contract wallets returning
arbitrary data, wallet-timeout followed by late definite rejection, and
canonical-effect invalidation before immediate reorg reinclusion. Successful,
not-yet-finalized and reverted reinclusions cannot leave the old receipt
presented as canonical.

Combined verification and the VM source/schema installation receipt are added
below when complete. The delivery does not label local tests as a live alpha.

rollback. Read/development runtimes and tunnels are outside its write set.

matching service bindings. The new financial flags remain OFF until the
actual identities and dependencies exist.

deployment is promoted into an operating profile. The ordinary Chainlink
feed mapping alone does not qualify the strict event-time price coverage.
See the runtime, executor, V4 execution and owner recovery documents for the
actual supported boundaries and required profiles.
