# Gas units on the deployed Domino V1 contracts — 22 September 2026

The activation checklist asked for the automation configuration's gas units to be
re-measured on the deployed contracts instead of carried over from the
[21 September study](GAS_FUNDING_STUDY_2026-09-21.md), which measured a fresh stack on a
fork. This record does that, on the mainnet addresses of the
[V1 release](../docs/deployments/v1-robinhood-mainnet-2026-09-21/deployment.json), and
records two things the measurement found on the way: the pool's protocol fee, which the
evidence builder refused, and the deployed engine's refusal of the study's swap recipe.

Everything here ran on a disposable Anvil child forked from the public endpoint
(`rpc.mainnet.chain.robinhood.com`) at the head block, from a development machine. No
transaction, key or address left the child; the deployed operator, publisher and guardian
were impersonated on the child only. As in the study, units are Anvil receipts under the
explicit ArbSys emulation, not live Nitro estimates: the emulation reproduces the child's
`NUMBER`/`BLOCKHASH`, not Nitro's opcode gas overrides.

## Method

`reports/gas-units-v1-2026-09-22/measure_v1_costs.py` is the study's fork experiment
(`backend/qualify_execution_fork.py`) with one difference: nothing is deployed but the
swap driver. The factory, read helper, routing helper, price guard, engine, implementation
and setup are adopted at their mainnet addresses after their runtime code hashes are read
on the fork and compared with the release manifest, the guard's publisher and band (50
sqrt-bps) and the engine's links are checked, and the vault is created through the
deployed factory on the real WETH/USDG pool (`0x84bd…ad0b6`), root ±200 ticks around the
spot with 2 WETH and 8,000 USDG. Forty round trips of the driver earn fees; then the
study's scenarios run: an honest direct compound, the honest swap recipe, the lifecycle
(successor MINT and every owner exit), and a redirect of fresh root fees into a minted
child. Every measured transaction is recorded with its kind, calldata size and the
source's `NodeInterface.gasEstimateL1Component` quote.

Two runs, at two head blocks, so that the variance between pool states is visible:

## Finding 1: the pool charges a protocol fee, and the evidence builder refused it

The live WETH/USDG pool's slot0 reads `protocolFee 213044` (52 pips each direction) and
`lpFee 200`. `automation_evidence.simulate_exact_input` refused any pool with a non-zero
protocol fee (`automation_pool_fee_unqualified`), so on this pool the cycle could never
build evidence, quote a swap or act: every pass would have ended in that refusal. The
fixture pool of the integrated tests has no protocol fee, which is why none of them saw it.

The fix composes the fee exactly as the core does (`Pool.swap`,
`ProtocolFeeLibrary.calculateSwapFee`): the direction's protocol share plus the LP fee
minus their product over 10⁶, charged on the input; fee growth, and therefore the
sampler's fee rate, stays the LP fee's, since the protocol's share never reaches the
position. A dynamic-fee pool (LP fee different from the key's static fee) is still
refused, as is an invalid protocol fee. Proof: `test_automation_evidence.py` checks the
composition against the core's formula (52 | 52 with 200 gives 252) and the walk against
the core's step math in both directions; `test_automation_cycle_evm.py` sets the same
52 | 52 protocol fee on the fixture pool and replays the automation's own swap quote
against the engine's `FeesConverted` output, which must match exactly. With the LP fee
alone that replay fails by 235,810,342 units of output, so the composition is what the
test pins.

## Finding 2: the study's swap recipe is refused by the deployed engine

`swap-honest` in both runs reverted with `InvalidAmount()`. The recipe sells half of the
net currency0 fee and adds a quarter of the feasible liquidity; the deployed engine
requires the swap's output to be consumed by the addition (the rule the automation's plan
builder sizes for at the post-swap price, recorded in the cycle's delivery report). The
refusal is the deployed contract behaving as specified, not a measurement of the swap
path. The swap unit therefore comes from the automation's own plan, in the rehearsal.

## Rehearsal: the automation cycle itself on the fork

`reports/gas-units-v1-2026-09-22/measure_v1_cycle.py` runs `SequenceCycle` end to end
against the fork: the guard's publisher rotated to a synthetic key through the
impersonated deployed guardian, the vault created through the deployed factory with a
synthetic operator, a disposable PostgreSQL with migrations 015, 019, 028 and 029, real
Chainlink references (ETH / USD and USDG / USD on 4663), `l1DataComponent:
"attested_none"` because the child has no `NodeInterface`, and the service's own
observe → sample → decide → publish → execute → reconcile → ledger path. Its cost records
are the gas figures; the planner chooses the action.

Evidence: [`v1-fork-cycle.json`](gas-units-v1-2026-09-22/v1-fork-cycle.json), fork block
69,710,393, with every pass's decision (reasons, evidence reasons, observation diagnostics,
sample count, the chosen candidate's action and whether it swapped).

| Cycle | Policy trigger | What the planner chose | Execution gas | Publication gas | Calldata |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `direct` | 1 % | `compound_self`, no swap, NET path (wire action 1) | 1,007,693 | 157,278 | 1,764 B |
| `swap` | 1 % | `compound_self` with a swap, GENERAL path (wire action 0) | 1,376,165 | 157,278 | 1,956 B |
| `mint` | 10 % | `wait`: `no_economically_qualified_candidate` on every pass (11 passes) | | | |
| `fund` | 10 % | `wait`: `no_economically_qualified_candidate` on every pass (11 passes) | | | |

Both executed cycles went through the whole service path on the deployed runtime: real
references qualified the pool spot at every pass (`evidenceReasons: []`), the swap was
quoted with the protocol fee composed, sized by the plan builder at the post-swap price,
published under the deployed guard's 50 sqrt-bps band, executed by the deployed engine
from the synthetic operator's signature, reconciled at finality, and read back by the
ledger's reader (cursor advanced, no exceptions). This is the first end-to-end run of the
automation against the deployed contracts and the real pool; it is fork evidence, not a
mainnet transaction.

The swap execution's 1,376,165 is the automation's own swap plan, which the study's
recipe could not produce on this engine; it sits below the study's 1,440,762–1,497,579
for a fresh stack. The direct cycle took the NET path (1,007,693), cheaper than the
general path the fork tool measured (1,074,542–1,177,086); `executeGasUnits.direct` has
to cover the general path, which the planner uses when netting is not available.

The successor cycles never acted: with real base fees, the mint's 1.9 M units and the
configuration's `successorActiveFractionBps: 5000` haircut on a range that is not active,
none of the 27–30 successor candidates passed the economic gates at the fee volumes of
this rehearsal. That is the planner refusing correctly, not a harness failure; the mint
and redirect units come from the fork tool above.

Suggested `executeGasUnits.swap`: 1,520,000 (1,376,165 plus 10 %), with the same caveat
as the other units: the number to configure is the one the operator re-measures at
activation with the node's own `NodeInterface` quotes, and the envelope's `max_gas` stays
a separate ceiling.

## What this does not establish

Units under Nitro's real opcode gas, the L1 data component on a real inclusion, and the
economics at real fee volumes (the rehearsal's fees came from a driver trading the pool on
the fork). Nothing here was sent to the mainnet; `executionAvailable` is unchanged.
