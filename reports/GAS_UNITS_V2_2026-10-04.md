# Gas units of Domino V2 against V1, on one Robinhood fork — 4 October 2026

The question: does the gas V2 was built to save show up on the real chain's state, not only in the offline suites? This record measures both versions on the same fork, under the conditions of [V1's record of 22 September](GAS_UNITS_V1_2026-09-22.md).

**V2 is not deployed on Robinhood.** These are fork receipts, not mainnet transactions: no V2 execution has run on the chain yet.

## Method

**The child.** Each run is an owned Anvil child (`DepositFork`, cancun) forked at the head block from the own node (the clawber Nitro node through the SSH tunnel, a loopback source). Reads of the source are bounded. No transaction leaves the child. Units are the child's receipts, as V1's were.

**The same conditions as V1's study:**
- **Pool:** WETH/USDG 0.02% (`0x84bd…ad0b6`), the real pool, with its 52-pip protocol fee.
- **Root:** 2 WETH and 8,000 USDG, 200 ticks either side of the spot.
- **Fees:** forty round trips of the study's driver, each leg the sale that moves the price 20 bps.

**V2 on the child:**
- Every F0 component is deployed at its CREATE3 address by `backend/tests/v2_local_chain.deploy_v2`. The artifacts are the merge commit's (`559c509f`), bound to their source bytes. The source tree is `7a78b034…`, the release manifest's.
- Roles are synthetic keys. Each plan carries the synthetic publisher's signed observation of the child's spot.
- Plans are built as the integration suites build them: collect the root, fund from its fees, amounts left to the vault. The swap sells 40% of the root's WETH fees through the pool itself, at the floor the vault computes. The planner is not used, since it does not mint successors yet.
- Script: [`measure_v2_costs.py`](gas-units-v2-2026-10-04/measure_v2_costs.py).

**V1 on the same child state.** V1's own script ran again on the deployed V1 contracts, minutes after the V2 runs: [`measure_v1_costs_rerun.py`](gas-units-v2-2026-10-04/measure_v1_costs_rerun.py). It is the 22 September script with one import renamed (#289). Both versions accrued nearly the same fees from their forty round trips:
- V1: 0.0015615 WETH and 4.2122 USDG;
- V2: 0.0015605 WETH and 4.2096 USDG.

**The swap's own cost** is +45,728 gas on V2: the compound with a swap minus the one without.

**Owner exits** are not the same operation in the two versions:
- **V1:** one transaction per step: a child 71,958, the fee reserves 69,656 and 77,861, an untracked recovery 67,458.
- **V2:** `exitAll` returns the successor and every reserve in one transaction: 172,411.

**The L1 data component** is NodeInterface's quote from the own node for each transaction's calldata. It reads 0 at `latest` and 2.3k–4.8k L2 gas units at `safe`, for both versions: under 1% of an execution.

## Against the estimates

**The V1 audit's estimate (#282, 1 Oct).** It predicted −25 to −35% per NET cycle, and 300–500k less per mint and per vault creation. Measured:
- −51 to −57% per cycle;
- 1.04M less per mint;
- 0.7M less per vault creation.

**The offline suites (`docs/v2/INTEGRATION_TESTS.md`).** They undercount the real pool. The compound without a swap measured 403k there, about 432k with the transaction's base and calldata, against 569k on the fork. Three things the test world does not have:
- a real pool's cold storage;
- the v4 subscriber notification on every change of the root;
- the real USDG token's transfers.

**The V2 planner's prior** (`docs/v2/V2_AUTOMATION_BACKEND.md`) is 450,000 for a compound and +170,000 for a swap. It is low on the compound and high on the swap: about 570,000 and +50,000 here. The presimulation still measures each call before anything is signed. Only the first screening uses the prior.

## What this does not cover

- **One pool state.** The node is pruned, and V1's runs varied 10–15% across states.
- **Plans built like the suites', not by the V2 planner.** Its own plan sizes the swap from the pool's ratio, not 40% of the WETH fees.
- **The automation's whole pass.** That needs the journal and database, as V1's cycle rehearsal had.
- **Funding a successor from its parent.** The V2 planner does not do it yet, and V1's redirect was refused in today's harness, as on 22 Sep's first run.
- **Nitro's own opcode pricing.** V2 calls no ArbSys, and the child runs the EVM's schedule, as V1's measurement did.
